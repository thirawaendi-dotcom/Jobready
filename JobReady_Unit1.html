1. Know Yourself

Before you apply, ask yourself:

  • What am I good at?
  • What skills have I developed?
  • What experience can I show?
  • What type of work interests me?
  • What are my career goals?

Remember: University projects, internships, part-time jobs, volunteering, and activities can all provide useful evidence of your skills.

🚨 Don't Make This Mistake

“I’ll apply first and read the job description later.”

Job seekers often apply because the job title looks interesting. But if you do not understand the requirements, you may miss important information or fail to show relevant skills in your CV and interview.

Better approach: Read the job ad → highlight requirements → compare them with your skills → decide what evidence to show.
